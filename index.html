Why cheap leads cost more

A lead that never answers is the most expensive thing you can buy.

It is not the price that hurts. It is the twenty minutes on the phone, the drive across town, and the quote you wrote for someone who was never going to buy. Four things make the difference, and none of them are filters applied after the fact.

The honest version

Cheap leads are cheap for a reason.

Anyone can hand you 40 leads. Most will be tyre kickers, wrong numbers and people who filled in a form about free panels at 11pm. A lead count is the easiest number in this industry to sell you and the least useful one to be handed.

What matters is how many turned into a job. A lead nobody else is calling, answered while they still care, closes several times as often as one sold four ways. Same leads, same effort, very different week.

Which brings us to the metric everyone gets wrong

Cost per lead is a trap. Cost per job is what pays your rent.
