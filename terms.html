These terms govern the provision of a managed lead generation engine by LEADGEN RENTALS (ABN 83 894 208 171). By engaging us you agree to them.

1. What we provide

We provide a managed lead engine: a landing page for a specific trade, the brand behind it, and the Meta advertising campaigns that feed it, run by us. We own the engine, the brand, the domain, the page and the advertising accounts. You are engaging us for the engine's output and our management of it, not acquiring any of those assets, and none of them transfer to you during or after the engagement.

An engine is used by one client at a time. While your engagement is live, no other business is put on your engine.

This does not grant you any exclusive right to an area, a postcode, a suburb or a region. We may run other engines covering the same places. The area you give us determines which engine you are placed on and how your campaigns are targeted, and nothing more.

2. Fees and advertising spend are separate

You make two payments, to two different parties.

Our service fee is paid to us, monthly in advance, through Stripe. It is $1,250 + GST per month on the Engine plan, or as agreed in writing on a Custom plan. It does not include any advertising spend.

Your advertising spend is paid by you directly to Meta Platforms. We grant you access to the Meta advertising account that runs your engine; you add your own payment card to that account; Meta charges that card for your campaigns at Meta's own prices. We do not receive, hold, invoice, front, mark up or take any commission on that money, and you can see every charge in your own Meta billing. The Engine plan requires a committed minimum advertising budget of $50 per day for the duration of each cycle.

The advertising account remains ours. Your access is granted for the duration of the engagement and is removed when it ends, at which point you remove your payment card from it.

3. What a lead is, and how it is made exclusive

A lead means a form submission containing a name and an Australian mobile number, captured with named consent to be contacted by one identified service business.

Before the form is shown to a visitor, that visitor is assigned to exactly one client, and that client's business name is written into the consent line. Every lead delivered to you is exclusive to you and named to you. It is delivered to no other business, and it is never resold.

Assignment is per person and permanent. Once a person has been assigned to a client, every later visit and submission by that person stays bound to the same client, including on our other engines. A person is never reassigned. Where the same person submits again within 30 days, the later submission is treated as a duplicate of the first and is delivered to that same client or not at all. It is never delivered to a different business.

4. The 10 Quoted Jobs Guarantee

On the Engine plan, for each guarantee period, we guarantee your engine will produce at least 10 quoted jobs. Where it does not, our service fee for that period is refunded to you in full on a claim made under this section. It applies to every period, not only your first, and you keep every lead, quote and job the period produced. On a Custom plan what we commit to is set out in your scope document and the remedy is the same.

The guarantee period is 30 consecutive calendar days commencing on the date your advertising first goes live, not the date you paid us. Any suspension of advertising during that period does not extend or reset it.

A quoted job is a lead delivered through your engine for which you have issued a written quotation, recorded in your dashboard or evidenced from your own records. Our control over a lead ends when that lead confirms interest in response to the automated follow-up, and whether a written quotation then issues depends on your own sales activity, which we do not control. Accordingly a lead that returns a confirmation response during the period is treated as a quoted job whether or not you quote it. A lead that both confirms and is quoted is counted once.

A refund is conditional on each of the following throughout the period: advertising was funded continuously at or above the daily budget agreed at activation, paid by you directly to the advertising platform; the automated follow-up remained enabled and the number assigned to you remained connected; each delivered lead was contacted and worked by you, recorded in your dashboard; no change was made to any campaign, creative, budget or targeting without our prior agreement; and you complied with your other obligations under these terms.

A claim must be made in writing within 7 calendar days of the end of the guarantee period. We will assess it against the platform record and respond within 14 calendar days. This guarantee applies to our service fee only; advertising spend is paid by you to the advertising platform and is not refundable by us. Nothing in this section limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

We also guarantee that every lead delivered to you is exclusive to you and named to you, that assignment of a person to you is permanent as set out in section 3, that no other business is put on your engine while your engagement is live, and that your engine and its campaigns run for each period you have paid our fee for, provided your advertising budget is funded.

5. What we do not guarantee

We make no representation as to any specific revenue, sales or profit outcome. We do not guarantee any cost per lead, cost per quoted job or cost per job won; any number of leads; that any lead will answer; that any quotation will be accepted; or any figure above the guaranteed number in section 4. Performance is influenced by factors outside our control, including your pricing and competitive position, your sales process and conversion ability, your responsiveness to leads, market conditions, seasonal demand, local competition and your operational capacity.

6. Billing and refunds

Our service fee is billed month to month in advance through our payment processor, Stripe. We do not run a period that has not been paid for. Prices are shown exclusive of GST unless stated, and GST is added at checkout where applicable.

Our service fee is refunded where a claim under the guarantee in section 4 is made and upheld. Otherwise period fees are not refundable, except where a refund is required by law. Advertising spend is paid by you to Meta and is not ours to refund in any circumstance, including where we miss the guarantee.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time before your next period begins, yourself. There is no lock in and no exit fee. Cancelling stops the next charge from us and does not refund the period you are in unless section 6 applies. Cancelling does not stop your advertising: Meta bills your card directly, so you must remove your payment card from the advertising account to stop your spend. On cancellation your engine is released and may be given to another business.

8. Acceptable use
