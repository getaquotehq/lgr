Rental Terms

Last updated 28 August 2026

The commercial terms for renting a slot on a lead engine. Plain English, no small-print games. These sit alongside our general Terms of Service and cover the money: what you are renting, what we guarantee, and - just as plainly - what we do not.

1. What you are renting

You are renting a slot on a lead engine - a landing page for one trade and area, plus the paid advertising campaigns behind it. You are not buying a fixed number of leads at a fixed unit price, and you are not buying the engine outright.

Renting a slot gives you no exclusive right to an area, a postcode, a region, or to the engine itself. The number of slots on an engine is fixed and limited.

What is exclusive is the lead. Every lead named to you is delivered to you and to nobody else. It is never resold, and never delivered twice.

2. How leads are made exclusive: named consent

Before the enquiry form is shown to a visitor, that visitor is assigned to exactly one renter and that renter's business name is written into the consent line. The homeowner consents to being contacted by that business, by name. The resulting lead is delivered to that business alone and is never resold.

Assignment is per person and it is permanent. Once a person has been assigned to a renter, every later visit and every later submission by that person remains bound to the same renter. A person is never reassigned. A homeowner is therefore never contacted by two of our renters about the same enquiry.

Where the same person submits more than once, the first submission is delivered as a lead and later submissions inside 30 days are treated as duplicates of it. They are delivered to the same renter or not at all; they are never delivered to anyone else.

Not every lead passes through the branded landing page directly. Some arrive via advertising-platform lead forms (for example, Meta/Facebook Lead Ads) that mirror the landing page's fields and consent wording rather than the page itself. Branding, layout and the offer stay identical to what you have seen; only the platform's native form presentation may differ. It does not change assignment, exclusivity or postcode matching.

3. Volume is not guaranteed

We do not guarantee any number of leads, any minimum number of leads, any cost per lead, or any outcome, on any tier. There is no floor, no volume commitment and no refund tied to volume.

Lead volume moves with the advertising auction, the season and market conditions. In good conditions your fee produces more leads; in poor conditions it produces fewer. We would rather tell you that plainly than quote you a number padded for our worst month.

Starter, Growth and Scale are service levels describing how much campaign weight sits behind your slot. They are not a commitment to spend any stated amount, and they are not a lead count.

4. What we do commit to

We commit to the things within our control, and only those:

a. Exclusivity. Every lead named to you is delivered to you and to no other business.
b. Named consent. Your business is named on the consent line of every lead delivered to you, and assignment of a person to you is permanent as described in section 2.
c. The engine runs. Your engine and its campaigns run for each period you have paid for.

We do not commit to lead volume, cost per lead, or whether any homeowner answers, quotes or converts. That part is the market and your sales process, not our product.

5. Lead quality, and why there are no replacements

We do not operate a lead-replacement or lead-credit scheme. Duds are handled by keeping them out of the system rather than compensating you afterwards. Before a lead is ever delivered, every one of the following runs server-side:

- Every mobile is checked against the live carrier networks. Disconnected numbers, landlines and invented digits are rejected outright and never become a lead.
- Hidden honeypot fields and completion-time checks drop bot and scripted submissions.
- Known disposable and temporary email domains are blocked.
- Persistent device and network fingerprinting with escalating cooldowns stops serial form-fillers.
- The postcode is matched against your service area before delivery. Out-of-area enquiries are not delivered.
- Duplicate phone numbers and email addresses to the same business are blocked. You are never delivered the same homeowner twice.

A submission that fails any of these is not delivered as a lead.

6. Fees, and that they are final

Slots are billed month to month in advance through Stripe. We do not begin running a period we have not been paid for. Prices are shown exclusive of GST unless stated; GST is added at checkout where applicable.

Period fees are non-refundable. There are no refunds for a change of mind, for a period that produced fewer leads than you hoped, for leads you chose not to call, for leads that did not answer, or for leads that did not convert. Because we do not promise volume, a low-volume period is not a breach of these terms and does not give rise to a refund.

Nothing in this section limits any right you have under the Australian Consumer Law, including where a service is not supplied at all or is not supplied with due care and skill.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time before your next period begins, yourself, without a phone call. There is no lock-in, no exit fee and no notice period. Cancelling stops the next charge; it does not refund the period you are already in. On cancellation your slot is released and may be taken by another business.

8. Acceptable use

You agree to contact homeowners only about the enquiry they submitted, to honour any request to stop contact, and to comply with the Spam Act, the Do Not Call Register and the Australian Privacy Principles. Misuse of delivered contact details is grounds for immediate termination without refund.

9. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability in connection with the service is limited to the fees paid for the affected billing cycle. Nothing in these terms excludes rights that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms, or to elect your option under section 3, email contact@leadgenrentals.com.au.
