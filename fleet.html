Where each payment goes

You pay us $1,250 + GST / month

Our fee for owning and running the engine, writing and testing the ads and managing the spend. It contains no advertising spend at all. Billed by card, monthly in advance, cancel any time.

You pay Meta $50 / day

Your advertising. We give you access to the ad account that runs your engine and you put your own card on it, so Meta bills you directly at Meta's prices. We never handle it, never invoice it and never mark it up. You can see every charge in your own billing.

If either number ever appears on its own, it is wrong. The fee is not the cost of advertising with us, and the budget is not a payment to us.

Is an engine free where you work?

An engine runs for one business at a time, so this is a real question with a real answer. Give us the postcode you want work in and we will tell you now. You are not buying that postcode and nobody gets exclusive rights to an area. It is just how we know which engine to put you on.
