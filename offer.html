Why quoted jobs and not leads

Anyone can hand you 40 leads.

Most of them will be tyre kickers, wrong numbers and people who filled in a form about free panels at 11pm. A lead count is the easiest number in this industry to hit and the least useful one to be handed, which is exactly why everyone guarantees it.

A quoted job is different. To get to ten, the enquiries have to be real people with real jobs who answered and let you price the work. You cannot pad your way there with volume, so that is the number we put our own fee behind.

And we count it fairly. Our job ends when a homeowner replies to the follow-up saying yes, they want a quote. Whether you then get the paperwork out is your sales process, not ours. So a lead that confirms counts as a quoted job whether or not you quoted it. We are not going to miss a guarantee because you had a busy week.

Straight answers

What people ask before they start.

What exactly counts as a quoted job?

Either of two things. A written quotation you issued to a delivered lead, recorded in your dashboard or evidenced from your own records. Or a delivered lead that replied to the automated follow-up confirming they want a quote or an appointment, whether or not you ended up quoting them.

The second one matters. Our control ends at the confirmation; what happens next is your sales process, and we should not be judged on it. A lead that both confirms and gets quoted counts once, not twice.

It counts whether or not the homeowner buys. We promised to put you in front of real people, not to close them for you. Only leads your engine delivered are counted, so your own referrals and repeat customers do not count towards it, or against it.

Why do I pay Meta instead of paying you for ads?

So you can see what your advertising actually cost. When an agency takes your budget into their own account, the only record you get is the number they report back. Here your card sits on the ad account, Meta bills you directly at Meta's prices, and you read your own invoice.

There is no markup because there is nothing for us to mark up, and no incentive for us to inflate a budget because we do not earn a cent on it.

Whose ad account is it, and what happens if I leave?

It is ours. We own the engine, the landing page, the brand behind it and the ad account that runs it, and you have access for as long as you are with us. You do not get edit rights, for the reason in the conditions above.

If you leave, you take your card off the account and we take the access off. The leads you were given stay yours, along with every conversation, quote and job in your dashboard. The engine was never yours and does not go with you, which is exactly why there is nothing for you to build, hand over or maintain.

What if the guarantee is missed because of something I did?

If you breach one of the conditions in a way that materially affects delivery, the guarantee does not apply to that period, and we will tell you in writing exactly what the breach was. We will not withhold a remedy without giving you that reason.

The conditions are all things you control, and there are only four. We are not looking for a technicality to escape on: if something went wrong at our end, the refund is yours.

Am I locked in?

No. Month to month, billed in advance, cancel any time before your next cycle starts. No lock-in, no exit fee, no notice period, and you can do it yourself in the dashboard.

Cancelling stops what you pay us. You stop your advertising by taking your card off the ad account, because that money was never ours.
