Why this exists

Every lead you buy, someone else is pocketing the spread.

A reseller buys enquiries and sells them on to you at a markup. When their costs drop they keep the difference. When their costs rise, either your price goes up or your lead gets sold to more people. You lose either way, and you never see the upside. Pay-per-lead is a toll, not a partnership.

FAQ

The questions service businesses actually ask.

Why isn't it a fixed cost per lead?

Because a fixed cost per lead only ever protects us. Ad platforms don't charge a flat rate - what it costs to reach a homeowner changes with the season and with demand. A per-lead reseller pockets the difference when costs fall and quietly shares your lead harder when they rise. Either way you carry the market and never see the upside.

We do it the other way around: you pay one flat, prepaid fee for the cycle and we run the campaigns underneath it. When costs are good, that fee buys more leads. When they spike, it buys fewer - we are not going to pretend otherwise, and we are not going to water down the lead to hit a number we promised you. The fee is fixed and known; the volume moves with the market.

What does "exclusive" actually mean here?

It means every lead goes to one service business - you - and nobody else. Ever. Not sold to three of your competitors, not resold a week later as an "aged" lead, not handed to a marketplace.

And we don't ask you to take that on trust. Every homeowner consents to be contacted by your business, by name, at the moment they submit the form. The exclusivity is built in at the moment they submit, not promised afterwards. There is nowhere else the lead can go.

How are leads delivered?

Three ways, simultaneously. The moment a lead comes in you get an SMS to your phone, an email to your inbox, and it appears inside your Lead Gen Rentals HQ dashboard - so you can see every lead in real time from any device. If you run a CRM we can push directly into that as well. Most service businesses use all four so nothing slips through.

On top of that, our AI texts the homeowner in your name within about 60 seconds of them enquiring - so by the time the lead reaches you, the conversation's already warm.

Is there any guarantee I'll get leads?

No, and we want to be blunt about that rather than bury it. We do not guarantee a lead count, a cost per lead, or any outcome. Nobody can honestly guarantee volume from an ad auction they do not control, and a company that does is either padding the number for its worst month or planning to share your lead harder when the month goes badly. What we do guarantee is the part we fully control: every lead named to you is delivered to you and to no other business, and your engine runs for every period you have paid for. You also see the campaign activity and the leads delivered side by side in your dashboard, so a quiet month is visible rather than explained away.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. It's month to month, billed in advance. Cancel any time before your next cycle starts - no lock-in, no exit fee, no notice period, and you can do it yourself. Cancelling frees your slot. If it's not working for you, you leave; the leads are what keep you, not a contract.
